Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries across the Group, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, creative ideas in agile teams. Various activities are bundled in our Corporate Functions division. In addition to our finance and IT services, this area also includes global marketing and sales management as well as human resources. Every day fresh ideas are created here, which together we lead to success. Personal responsibility and networked work are particularly important to us. Do you also want to participate? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • You design, design and realise packaging designs for our year-round and seasonal range – from the first idea to the final production
  • Develop new packaging concepts and adapt existing designs for different products, formats and international markets
  • You implement designs technically and create ready-to-print production data based on punched cracks and technical specifications, including pure drawing and prepress
  • You ensure the design and technical quality and check proofs, print data, colors, refinements and production templates
  • You are responsible for artwork and versioning management including language adaptations, correction loops as well as mandatory information and declarations in coordination with the specialist areas
  • You work closely with product management, retail marketing, purchasing, production as well as international brand managers, agencies and production partners and ensure compliance with brand governance
  • You will continuously develop our corporate design and design new design elements such as patterns, icons, pictograms and illustrations.
  • You develop and adapt creative design concepts for other brand touchpoints such as print and digital media, non-food, visual merchandising, signalling and 3D branding and professionally prepare image material from product photo shoots

You bring that with you

  • You have completed training as a polygraph:in or as a graphic/communication designer:in (Swiss diploma)
  • You bring 5–7 years of professional experience in graphic and packaging design, ideally in the premium, FMCG, food, retail or agency environment
  • You have in-depth experience in the design and implementation of packaging, including stamping cracks, pure drawing, print data preparation and production support
  • You master the Adobe Creative Cloud (especially Illustrator, InDesign and Photoshop) and MS Office securely
  • You have a very good understanding of printing processes, colors, materials and refinements as well as a high demand on technical quality
  • You have experience in the design of print and digital formats and convince through creativity, conceptual strength and a strong sense of typography, composition, brand aesthetics and high-quality product design
  • You develop brand worlds creatively and at the same time guideline-compliant and convince through a structured, independent and reliable working method as well as sovereign stakeholder management
  • You communicate fluently in German and English

This is what we offer you

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • The opportunity to bring in fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Internal and external training
  • Individual career opportunities
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Chocolate in the workplace
  • Free parking
  • Discounts in Läderach Café and staff restaurant

Own it. Grow it. Make it fresh.
