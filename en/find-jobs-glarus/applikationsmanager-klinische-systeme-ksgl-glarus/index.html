Your opportunities with us

  • You assume application responsibility for the KISIM clinical information system and other clinical application systems and are responsible for the operation, configuration and ongoing development
  • Ensure second-level support and analyze and solve complex disruptions and user concerns
  • you act as an interface between the clinical departments, technical IT and external system suppliers and analyze and optimize the processes
  • you lead or work in digitization and application projects in the clinical environment and support intercompany specialist and steering committees
  • you are responsible for the authorization concepts and their implementation in the clinical system environment
  • You design and conduct application training and create application documentation
  • you accompany system releases, including test coordination and acceptance
  • You support on- and off-boarding in the area of clinical documentation

You are fit for us if

  • you have completed training in healthcare and/or computer science, advantageously supplemented by further training in medical informatics or equivalent;
  • You are ready for further training in this area
  • you have experience in the operation and support of clinical information systems, ideally KISIM
  • You can score with a good understanding of clinical processes in medicine and care
  • you have already gained experience in project work and you appreciate the cooperation with different professional groups
  • distinguish your independent, reliable and structured working method and your strong communication skills

We reward your passion

  • with a meaningful job, because with your work you support our employees in concentrating on the essentials – the care of our patients
  • with varied tasks, including great personal responsibility in a modern hospital
  • with a motivated, collegial and helpful computer science team
  • with a modern IT landscape with exciting tasks and development opportunities
  • with the possibility to actively shape processes and contribute your ideas
  • with flexible working conditions and contemporary employment conditions
  • with a wide range of training opportunities

The Cantonal Hospital Glarus is located in the middle of the spectacular Glarner mountains – just one hour from Zurich. Here the high quality of life is combined with numerous opportunities for mountain sports and outdoor activities. With 65 acute medicine beds and six in the intensive care unit, the Cantonal Hospital Glarus AG offers a broad range of disciplines. As a medical competence centre, the hospital provides extended basic care for the Canton of Glarus and the adjacent regions.
