Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries across the Group, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, creative ideas in agile teams. Various activities are bundled in our Corporate Functions division. In addition to our finance and IT services, this area also includes global marketing and sales management as well as human resources. Every day fresh ideas are created here, which together we lead to success. Personal responsibility and networked work are particularly important to us. Do you also want to participate? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • Develop and manage seasonal retail marketing campaigns for over 250 stores worldwide, translating global branding and marketing strategies into commercially successful in-store experiences
  • You will be responsible for projects from idea to conception, prototyping and production to international rollout, combining creative, commercial and operational requirements
  • You work closely with product marketing, international markets as well as external agencies, suppliers and production partners and lead complex projects to success in a structured and reliable manner.
  • Join international markets for seasonal activations, new openings and retail initiatives, and develop solutions that combine local requirements with a consistent premium brand experience
  • You analyze feedback from the stores, derive concrete optimization measures and continuously develop campaigns, visual merchandising concepts and processes.
  • You support the further development of our global store concept and transfer it to different retail formats such as flagship stores, kiosks or shop-in-shop concepts

You bring that with you

  • You have a completed Bachelor in Marketing, Business Administration or a similar field and are enthusiastic about retail marketing and in-store communication. A parallel master's degree should not prevent you from applying
  • You bring your first professional experience in marketing, retail or project management and have a good feeling for brand-appropriate and commercially effective implementations
  • You convince with strong organizational skills and a high level of detail orientation in order to control several topics in a structured and reliable manner in parallel
  • You have fluent German and English skills in words and writing and enjoy working in an international environment
  • You are characterized by your own initiative, reliability and a hands-on mentality as well as the motivation to actively promote topics and successfully implement them.

This is what we offer you

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • The opportunity to bring in fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Internal and external training
  • Individual career opportunities
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Chocolate in the workplace
  • Free parking
  • Discounts in Läderach Café and staff restaurant

Sounds interesting? Then apply to us. Emilija Minova, Head of Talent Acquisition & Employer Branding, is happy to help you with any questions.
