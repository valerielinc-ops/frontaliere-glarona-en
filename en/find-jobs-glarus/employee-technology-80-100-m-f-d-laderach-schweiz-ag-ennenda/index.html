Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries across the Group, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, innovative ideas in agile teams. In our production, we produce fresh couvertures from high-quality raw materials. Afterwards, we process and refine the liquid chocolates in our chocolate factory to form FrischSchoggi, pralinés, truffes, figures and much more. Do you also want to participate? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • You optimize existing manufacturing processes to continuously improve the quality of our products
  • You lead and accompany technological projects in all areas of production
  • You plan, coordinate and carry out tests directly on the production facilities
  • You support product development in the feasible design and introduction of new products
  • You work closely with product development, production, continuous improvement and quality management
  • You strengthen technological know-how and support production in quality and efficiency optimizations
  • You will become an active member of our internal sensor team

You bring that with you

  • You have a degree in food technology, food sciences (FH/ETH) or a comparable qualification
  • You bring several years of experience from the food industry, ideally in the chocolate industry, and have already successfully led projects for process and quality optimization
  • You work in a structured, analytical, solution-oriented manner and have a strong quality awareness
  • Keep up-to-date with the latest technological developments and manufacturing processes and work independently, prioritize and bring a strong hands-on mentality
  • You have excellent communication and cooperation skills for cross-departmental cooperation and are happy to take on responsibility
  • You have a great passion for the product chocolate
  • You speak fluent German and ideally bring good English skills

This is what we offer you

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • The opportunity to bring in fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Internal and external training
  • Individual career opportunities
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Chocolate in the workplace
  • Free parking
  • Discounts in Läderach Café and staff restaurant

Own it. Grow it. Make it fresh.
