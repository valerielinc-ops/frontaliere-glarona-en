Full description

Läderach (Switzerland) AG is a Swiss chocolate manufacturer that produces high-quality fresh chocolate. Founded in 1962, the family-owned company today employs over 2,500 people worldwide, from more than 80 countries. Our headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In this picturesque location, surrounded by the Alps, we produce our fresh chocolates and develop new creative ideas in agile teams. In our 'Corporate Functions' department, various activities are grouped together. In addition to our financial and IT services, this department also includes global marketing and sales management, as well as human resources. Every day, here, new ideas are created that we bring to fruition together. Personal responsibility and networked working are particularly important to us. Do you also want to be a part of this? Then come to us. You can expect:

  • Implement and monitor safety and health protection measures, and contribute to their continuous improvement.
  • Conduct risk analyses, accompany safety inspections, and clarify accidents.
  • Advise and support the operational management in all safety and health protection matters in accordance with the EKAS 6508 directive.
  • Teach and sensitize employees and managers on relevant safety topics.
  • Actively collaborate with audits, inspections, and official checks.
  • Maintain and develop safety concepts, guidelines, and documentation, and continuously develop them.
  • You have a completed vocational training and a further education in the field of safety and health protection (e.g. as a safety officer or similar).
  • You have several years of work experience in an industrial environment.
  • You are familiar with the EKAS 6508 directive and the relevant statutory regulations in depth.
  • You have strong communication skills, determination, and a sense of things, and work willingly with different categories of people.
  • You work independently, structured, and solution-oriented. -
