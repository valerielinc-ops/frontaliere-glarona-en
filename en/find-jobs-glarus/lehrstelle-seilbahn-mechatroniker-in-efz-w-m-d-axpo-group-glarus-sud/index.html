Full description

As a ropeway mechatronics technician, you are a professional in securing the smooth operation of cable cars, aerial cable cars and gondola cars. Start of teaching: 1 August 2027

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Maintenance of equipment and operating equipment, e.g. ropes, rollers, brakes, etc.
  • Testing of sub-installations using measuring instruments
  • Maintain checklists and create reports
  • Understanding weather and avalanche reports and identifying potential hazards
  • Ensuring the power supply and, if necessary, taking measures

Profile and skills

  • Completed elementary school
  • Enjoying accurate and careful working methods
  • Interest in technical relationships and mechanics
  • Physical capacity and serenity in hectic situations
  • Curiosity, perseverance and good concentration ability

About the team

As a learner of cable car mechatronics, you work with us in a well-established and future-oriented team.

Start your apprenticeship with an eventful and practical introductory week together with many other learners. You can also expect 6 weeks of holidays, a contribution to your mobile subscription and a generous mobility contribution for your flexibility on the road. In addition, we reward good performance with great extras.

Look forward to a dynamic environment where you can get involved and grow beyond yourself.
