Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries across the Group, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, creative ideas in agile teams. Various activities are bundled in our Corporate Functions division. In addition to our finance and IT services, this area also includes global marketing and sales management as well as human resources. Every day fresh ideas are created here, which together we lead to success. Personal responsibility and networked work are particularly important to us. Do you also want to participate? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • You have overall responsibility for planning, controlling and implementing IT projects in the field of HR applications (e.g. SAP SuccessFactors, Payroll, Time Tracking, Recruiting, Learning) as well as corporate service applications (e.g. Finance, Legal, Facility Management, Collaboration Tools)
  • You proactively identify risks, dependencies and optimization potentials along the end-to-end processes (hire-to-retire, procurement-to-pay, record-to-report, etc.)
  • You work closely with HR, Finance and Corporate Functions and translate professional requirements into sustainable IT solutions
  • You control internal and external partners (e.g. implementation partners, SaaS providers)
  • You will establish and develop agile project and delivery models (Scrum, Kanban, hybrid approaches)
  • Ensure high data quality, integration stability and user experience across system boundaries
  • You communicate projects and stakeholders transparently up to management and board level
  • You plan and run release, change and go-live activities including business readiness and adoption measures
  • You will continuously improve project methods, governance structures and toolsets
  • You ensure compliance with compliance, data protection and security requirements (e.g. GDPR, SOX, audit requirements)

You bring that with you

  • You have completed your studies in (economic) computer science or a comparable qualification as well as several years of experience as an IT project manager in the field of HR-IT and/or corporate service applications
  • You have a very good understanding of HR and corporate processes as well as their IT-side mapping and have experience with SaaS solutions, integration scenarios and complex stakeholder landscapes
  • You have in-depth knowledge of agile and classic project management methods (Scrum, Kanban, PMI, PRINCE2, SAFe) and use them situationally
  • You work hands-on, take on a high level of personal responsibility and convince with implementation strength as well as a structured, analytical and solution-oriented working method
  • You have very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing and communicate safely with specialist areas, IT and management
  • You think proactively ahead, drive topics independently and react flexibly to changes as well as transformation and digitalization initiatives

This is what we offer you

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • The opportunity to bring in fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Internal and external training
  • Individual career opportunities
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Chocolate in the workplace
  • Free parking
  • Discounts in Läderach Café and staff restaurant

Sounds interesting? Then apply to us. First name, position, telephone number, is available for questions.

We ask for your understanding that we do not consider external recruitment agencies when filling this position.
