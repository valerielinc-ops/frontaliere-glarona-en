Full description

For Raiffeisenbank Glarnerland, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Näfels and Glarus, we are looking for a service-oriented, initiative and dynamic personality as Head of Credit Officer (f/m/d) to strengthen our team.

Tasks

  • Professional and personnel management of the credit office team as well as promotion and further development of employees
  • Analysis, assessment and approval of credit transactions in the private and corporate customer segment within the jurisdictional order
  • Sparring partner for customer advisors, bank management and other stakeholders in complex financing, risk and approval issues
  • Responsibility for monitoring credit risks, regulatory compliance and ensuring high data quality
  • Responsibility for technical topics in the credit area, participation in projects and reporting to bank management and board of directors

Requirements

  • Banking education with higher professional training in finance or banking
  • Several years of in-depth experience in the lending business of a Swiss bank as well as proven competence in private and corporate banking
  • Very good knowledge in the analysis, assessment and management of credit risks as well as the relevant regulatory requirements
  • Leadership experience is beneficial
  • Experience in estimation and ideally knowledge of tools such as Wüest Dimensions
