Full description

In the idyllic landscape of Glarnerland is our renowned institution, the Braunwald Rehaklinik. We focus on the treatment of people with psychiatric and psychosomatic diseases. For the satisfaction of our guests and patients, our valued specialists provide high-quality and diverse services every day.

Your tasks

  • Menu preparation: Responsible for preparing our fresh menus for patients and employees (banquets, à la carte). This also includes the preparation of the dishes as well as cooperation during service hours
  • Dietary cooking Compliance with the food regulations and safety regulations for patients and ensure the production of food according to meal regulations
  • Hygiene: Compliance with the hygiene regulations according to GHP and HACCP
  • Training: Support our apprentices with their training through your cooperation

Your profile

  • Training: Completed training as a cook / cook
  • Experience: You already bring experience in the à la carte area and advantage in the hospital kitchen and know the classic and modern cuisine

  • Professional appearance: Careful appearance, as there is close contact with the guests and patients
  • Language: Very good knowledge of German
