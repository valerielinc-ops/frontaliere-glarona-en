Full description

Your opportunities with us

  • You are the first contact person for our employees and take over the first and second level support – by phone, via the ticket system and on site
  • you analyse disturbances, repair them independently or coordinate the further processing with the competent specialist bodies
  • You manage around 650 computer workstations as well as our clinical and administrative applications and ensure smooth operation
  • You install, configure and maintain hardware, software, network components and communications and peripherals
  • Ensure that hardware, software, license and inventory data is kept complete and up-to-date
  • You will advise our employees competently on IT issues, recognize their needs and find pragmatic, sustainable solutions
  • you are actively involved in exciting computer science projects and contribute your ideas to the further development of our IT environment

You are fit for us if

  • you have completed training as a computer scientist EFZ (platform development, company informatics or ICT specialist) or a comparable training with corresponding professional experience
  • you have already gained experience in IT support and support of Windows client environments
  • You ideally have knowledge of Microsoft 365, Active Directory, Intune/MECM or similar technologies
  • Knowledge of the ERP system Opale is also advantageous
  • You enjoy contact with people and understand yourself as a service provider – for you, the needs of our employees are the focus
  • you communicate at any time, even in demanding situations, friendly, understandable and at eye level
  • you work independently, reliably and in a structured way and keep an overview even when things get hectic
  • distinguish you team spirit, initiative and the willingness to learn new things

We reward your passion

  • with a varied activity, including great personal responsibility in a modern hospital
  • with a motivated, collegial and helpful computer science team
  • with a modern IT landscape with exciting tasks and development opportunities
  • with the possibility to actively shape processes and contribute your ideas
  • with flexible working conditions and contemporary employment conditions
  • with a wide range of training opportunities

The Cantonal Hospital Glarus is located in the middle of the spectacular Glarner mountains – just one hour from Zurich. Here the high quality of life is combined with numerous opportunities for mountain sports and outdoor activities. With 65 acute medicine beds and six in the intensive care unit, the Kantonsspital Glarus AG offers a broad range of disciplines. As a medical competence centre, the hospital provides extended basic care for the Canton of Glarus and the adjacent regions.
