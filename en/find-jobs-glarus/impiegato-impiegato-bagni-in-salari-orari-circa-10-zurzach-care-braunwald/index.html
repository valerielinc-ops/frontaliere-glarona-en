Full description

In the idyllic landscape of Glarnerland is our renowned institution, the Braunwald Rehaklinik. We focus on the treatment of people with psychiatric diseases. For the satisfaction of our guests and patients, our valued specialists provide high-quality and diverse services every day.

Are you flexible and looking for a small side job? Then you are exactly right with us! The job is also perfect for students, temporary workers and people looking for a smaller workload. We look forward to your application via our online tool!

Your tasks

  • Supervision : Responsible for bathing supervision in Aqualpina by complying with bathing rules to ensure safety
  • Support Therapy: Perform heat therapy in the bath on behalf of physiotherapy
  • Orders: Required orders for the bathroom and the MTT area
  • Maintenance: notification and agreement with the technical service regarding system failures

Your profile

  • Flexibility: The work assignments are distributed over the week, also weekend assignments are desired
  • Personality: You show empathy, openness and a strong

Communication ability in dealing with our patients and with the entire team

  • Languages: You bring good German skills in word and writing
  • IT skills: You bring basic knowledge with the common Microsoft programs (Word, Excel, Outlook, etc.)

Your benefits with us

  • Mobility grant: Financial support for travelling by Braunwaldbahn
  • Working environment: Working in an idyllic, car-free recreation area in an appreciative and motivated team
  • Culinary offer: daily three freshly cooked menus at special prices and once a month a breakfast offered by the group of companies
  • Wellness: Free use of medical-therapeutic training and twice a week of the bathing area
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Pension fund contributions: above-average participation and management costs with attractive pension options
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, pharmacies as well as our membership at Benefit@work and free admission and other discounts in the wellness spa FORTYSEVEN
