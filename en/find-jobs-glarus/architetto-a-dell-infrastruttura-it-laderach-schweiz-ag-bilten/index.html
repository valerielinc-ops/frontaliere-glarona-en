Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries across the Group, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, creative ideas in agile teams. Various activities are bundled in our Corporate Functions division. In addition to our finance and IT services, this area also includes global marketing and sales management as well as human resources. Every day fresh ideas are created here, which together we lead to success. Personal responsibility and networked work are particularly important to us. Do you also want to participate? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • You will define and develop the target architecture for our global IT infrastructure in the areas of network, data center, cloud, workplace, identity, monitoring, backup and disaster recovery
  • Translate business, security and operational requirements into clear architecture principles, standards, reference designs and technology roadmaps
  • You will be responsible for architectural and design audits and ensure that our solutions are in line with IT strategy, security and operational requirements
  • You will develop sustainable hybrid infrastructure solutions for our local environments, Microsoft Azure and our international locations
  • You support IT teams and project leaders in solution design, technology selection, implementation and the structured transfer to the company
  • Evaluate new technologies and partner solutions, manage technical risks and drive standardization, automation, simplification and cost efficiency

You bring that with you

  • You bring many years of experience in IT infrastructure and have profound experience in infrastructure or solution architecture
  • Experience developing secure and highly available infrastructure solutions in hybrid on-premises and cloud environments
  • You have in-depth knowledge in the creation of target architectures, standards, roadmaps and technical documentation
  • You are familiar with Security by Design, Zero Trust, Lifecycle Management, Resilience and IT Service Management and can translate these requirements into practical solutions
  • You convince with strong analytical and communication skills, secure stakeholder management and experience in evaluating technologies, solutions and external partners
  • You have a degree in computer science or information technology or a comparable practical qualification and communicate safely in German and English; relevant certifications in the areas of architecture, cloud, network, security or ITIL are advantageous

This is what we offer you

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • The opportunity to bring in fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Internal and external training
  • Individual career opportunities
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Chocolate in the workplace
  • Free parking
  • Discounts in Läderach Café and staff restaurant

Own it. Grow it. Make it fresh.

Sounds interesting? Then apply to us. Fabio Ferramosca, Talent Acquisition Partner, is at your disposal for any questions.
