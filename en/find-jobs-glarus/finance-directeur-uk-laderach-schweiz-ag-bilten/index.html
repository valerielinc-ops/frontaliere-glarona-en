Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries across the Group, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, creative ideas in agile teams. Various activities are bundled in our Corporate Functions division. In addition to our finance and IT services, this area also includes global marketing and sales management as well as human resources. Every day fresh ideas are created here, which together we lead to success. Personal responsibility and networked work are particularly important to us. Do you also want to participate? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • You take on an active role in the monthly group consolidation and gradually develop into a more comprehensive responsibility
  • You check and plausibility the reporting packages of our international companies and ensure high data quality
  • You are responsible for intercompany voting, eliminations and consolidation entries and analyze balance sheet, income statement, cash flow and equity
  • You will contribute to the consolidated financial statements and Group audit and work closely with our international finance teams
  • You will continuously develop our closing, consolidation and reporting processes and drive their standardization and automation
  • You will continue to develop LucaNet and our consolidation structures and thus support efficient and transparent Group Finance processes
  • You take financial responsibility for our UK company and ensure monthly and annual accounts as well as compliance with relevant accounting, tax and compliance requirements
  • You will analyse the financial development of our UK company, coordinate audit and intercompany issues and act as a sparring partner for local management

You bring that with you

  • You have a bachelor's degree in business administration or a comparable education
  • You bring at least five years of professional experience in finance
  • You have good knowledge of the Swiss GAAP FER accounting standards and can safely apply them in practice
  • You ideally bring experience with SAP FI/CO
  • You work proactively, independently and like to take on responsibility
  • You have fluent German and English skills in word and writing
  • You are characterized by a high willingness to learn, self-motivation and enthusiasm
  • You work results-oriented and keep track of complex financial topics

This is what we offer you

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • The opportunity to bring in fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Internal and external training
  • Individual career opportunities
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Chocolate in the workplace
  • Free parking
  • Discounts in Läderach Café and staff restaurant

Own it. Grow it. Make it fresh.

Sounds interesting? Then apply to us. Fabio Ferramosca, TA Partner, is at your disposal for any questions.
