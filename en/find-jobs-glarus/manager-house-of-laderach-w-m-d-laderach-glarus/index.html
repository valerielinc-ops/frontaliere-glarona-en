Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family-owned company today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, innovative ideas in agile teams. The “Retail” division comprises more than 250 chocolateries in more than 30 countries. Here we offer our chocolate specialties and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. Do you also want to participate? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • You run the House of Läderach as a unique visitor and experience centre with retail, museum, café, live production, walk-in studio and event area
  • You are responsible for budget, revenue, cost and profitability targets for the entire House of Läderach
  • You will continuously develop the business areas Retail, Museum & Guided Tours, Corporate Events, Courses, Café, Live Production, Walk-in Atelier and Welcome Desk and tap into new potential
  • You provide an outstanding customer experience across all areas
  • You define measures, manage cross-divisional projects and make a significant contribution to substantially increasing visitor numbers in the coming years.
  • You will work closely with the country team Switzerland and the HQ functions – in particular Retail Switzerland, Marketing, Controlling, Customer Service and Technical Service.
  • You ensure efficient processes as well as the highest quality, hygiene and safety standards
  • You analyze key figures, derive optimization measures and ensure the achievement of goals
  • You develop your managers and employees further and promote a performance- and service-oriented team culture

You bring that with you

  • You have a degree, preferably in business administration, marketing or business management, as well as several years of management experience in the areas of events, catering and / or sales
  • You have a strong business understanding and assume budget, process and result responsibility
  • You can communicate in German and English and inspire with your winning, charming manner
  • You convince with customer orientation, initiative, creativity, team spirit and pragmatic solutions
  • You get involved, like to work operationally and are ready to be regularly on weekends and public holidays
  • You love chocolate – and maybe you’re already a Läderach fan

This is what we offer you

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • The opportunity to bring in fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Internal and external training
  • Individual career opportunities
  • Attractive sales premium for joint achievement of goals
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Free parking
  • Chocolate in the workplace

Sounds interesting? Then apply to us. Emilija Minova, Head of Talent Acquisition & Employer Branding, is happy to help you with any questions.
