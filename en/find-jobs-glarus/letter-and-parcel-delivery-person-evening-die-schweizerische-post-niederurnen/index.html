Letter and parcel delivery person evening — Die Schweizerische Post

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Are you looking for an exciting part-time job where you can engage in physical activity? With your local knowledge you skilfully maneuver the van through all main and secondary roads of Niederurnen.

You can do that.

  • Together with your team, you prepare the delivery tour for the evening in the afternoon.
  • Thanks to you, legal certificates in your assigned territory arrive on time and reliably at the right addresses.
  • You use modern work equipment – like a smartphone – to scan the shipments.
  • After successful completion of the tour, you will ensure order in the hall and help with in-house work.
  • With your friendliness and reliability, you contribute to customer satisfaction.
  • Your assignments are daily from Monday to Friday from 15.30 to 20.00.

You bring that with you

  • Completed basic vocational training, preferably in the logistics industry, is an advantage.
  • You have the driver’s license of category B and like to travel outside in wind and weather.
  • Teamwork is important to you and you quickly have an overview geographically.
  • Your reliability, as well as independent and forward working methods distinguish you.
  • For this physically challenging activity, you are fit and like to actively participate. Confident knowledge of German in words and writing round off your profile.
