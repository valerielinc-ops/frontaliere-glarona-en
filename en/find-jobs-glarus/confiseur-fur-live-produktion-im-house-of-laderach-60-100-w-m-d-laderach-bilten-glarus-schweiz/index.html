Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries across the Group, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, innovative ideas in agile teams. The “Retail” division comprises more than 250 chocolateries in over 30 countries. Here we offer our chocolate specialties and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. Do you also want to participate? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • You like working with chocolate
  • You are actively involved in the production of chocolate products in live production
  • You make confiserie items by hand to your specifications and create special and seasonal chocolate products
  • You develop creative ideas and successfully implement them, support the team at live events and presentations
  • You advise and support customers with individual customer orders
  • You look after our guests in the Walk-in Atelier and are in direct contact with our visitors
  • You conduct courses on the subject of chocolate in our studio
  • You ensure compliance with quality standards and consistently implement the hygiene regulations according to HACCP

You bring that with you

  • You have professional experience in animation, gastronomy or hospitality
  • You have a creative sense and pleasure in putting ideas into practice – be it chocolate decorations or innovative concepts for live production
  • You are an extrovert who likes to contact guests
  • You have a passion for chocolate and a strong host mentality
  • You perform with a well-groomed appearance and professional appearance
  • You show high commitment and work strongly team-oriented
  • Your enthusiasm for work is contagious and creates a positive atmosphere for the guests
  • You have very good knowledge of German and English and convince by your strong communication strength

This is what we offer you

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • The opportunity to bring in fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Internal and external training
  • Individual career opportunities
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Possibility to enjoy chocolate in the workplace
  • Good accessibility of locations

Sounds interesting? Then apply to us. Annett Kraft, HoL Team Leader Live Production, is at your disposal for any questions.

We ask for your understanding that we do not consider external recruitment agencies when filling this position.
